Shared trader chat

Chat and crowd votes are free and live across every device — no accounts, no database to create. Pick a trader nickname below; it will appear on your messages and vote announcements.

Relay status: —. Every connected client syncs messages and votes in real time. Each trader gets one vote per 15-minute cycle on the asset they are viewing, and every vote is announced in chat automatically.

How the call is made

  • 01Nine models estimate whether the current 15-minute candle closes above its open — displacement, trend, reversion, order flow, pattern, follow-through, market odds, sentiment and cross-asset.
  • 02Each vote is converted to log-odds and pooled, weighted by regime fit and by that model's live accuracy. The pooled number is then recalibrated against every call this browser has already scored.
  • 03The call is written to the log five minutes into the candle, then scored on the exchange close. Weights and calibration shift after each resolution.

Market odds and chart targets use available current prediction-market data. Target lines show the market target level. Votes and shared chat do not alter the model.
